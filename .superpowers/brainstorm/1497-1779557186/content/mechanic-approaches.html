Which mechanic is the Zepto counter-move?

Three fundamentally different answers to "what happens in the 10-minute window?"

A
Freeze Frame — The Wait as Cinema Recommended
"Blinkit owns the scream. We own the ten minutes."
After ordering, the tracking screen becomes a full cinematic countdown — dramatic Zepto purple, live visual of your scoop racing through the city, temperature ticker proving it's still frozen. The final "YOUR ICE CREAM IS HERE" moment is screenshot-bait by design. The share artifact IS the wait screen. No separate mechanic needed.

Why it wins
Directly occupies the 10-minute canvas. Every order becomes a share moment. Blinkit literally cannot copy this without admitting their window is longer. Screenshot of the countdown = instantly Zepto.
Risk
Needs the UI to be extraordinary — a mediocre countdown doesn't go viral. The mechanic is UI-dependent. Requires tight animation and brand execution.
Reusability
"Your biryani is racing" → works for any time-sensitive impulse category.
B
The Scoop Report — Speed Made Shareable
"Your delivery, receipted."
After delivery, Zepto generates a branded "Scoop Report" card: exact delivery time, temperature reading, flavor, and a witty Zepto-voice line. It's Spotify Wrapped for your ice cream run — a beautiful, portrait-format card built for Instagram stories. The share moment is post-delivery, not during the wait.

Why it works
Lower execution risk — the share artifact is a generated card, not a live experience. Temperature data makes Zepto's speed claim concrete and tangible. Cards go viral on their own.
Risk
Requires temp sensors or integration — ops dependency. Post-delivery moment misses the excitement window. Less "theatre" than the brief asks for. Competitor-copyable.
Reusability
Works for any category with a measurable delivery promise.
C
Night Drop — FOMO at 10PM
"Catch the drop. Or miss it."
Between 9–11PM, a "Night Drop" appears on the home screen — curated ice cream combos, available for a 10-minute flash window, then gone. Users race to claim. The mechanic is FOMO × speed. Share artifact is "I caught the drop" screenshots. Streetwear culture applied to ice cream.

Risk
Doesn't directly counter Blinkit's moment — it's a different battle. Needs inventory/discount decisions (brief says no ops changes). Could feel like a sale, not a brand moment.
Reusability
Works for any impulse category with natural evening demand.
